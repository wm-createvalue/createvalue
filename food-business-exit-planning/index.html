Exit Planning

Build a food business that can be sold on your terms

Most food businesses aren't structured for an exit — the financials aren't clean, the story isn't buyer-ready, and the value hasn't been surfaced. We run a 24–36 month program to change that.

Start a Conversation ↗See Results
Three distinct services

Exit readiness, succession, and transaction preparation are not the same thing.

We are advisors, not business brokers — our role is to make the business more valuable and more transferable, and to prepare you for the negotiation, not to run the sale process itself.

Exit readiness

Making the business more transferable and valuable, well before a sale is on the table.

Succession planning

Reducing dependence on the owner so the business can run, and be valued, without you in every decision.

Transaction preparation

Preparing the information, narrative and negotiating position once a sale process is underway.

The 24–36 month program

What's included.

Step One

Financial normalization

Restructured so buyers see the business as it actually performs, not distorted by owner-specific decisions.

Step Two

Buyer-ready growth story

A strategic narrative and performance record that shows buyers where the value came from and where it's going.

Step Three

Buyer profile & pre-sale relationships

Identifying likely buyer profiles and beginning relationships well before a formal process starts.

Step Four

Structure, terms & timing

Guidance on deal structure, terms, timing, and — just as importantly — when to walk away.
