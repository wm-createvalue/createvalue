What the output connects

One integrated model, not a stack of disconnected assumptions.

Commercial

Market assumptions & gross margin

Grounded in what the market will actually support, not a template growth curve.

Capital

What the investment costs, and how it should be funded across debt, grants, and equity.

Operating

Operating capacity & working capital

Realistic utilization assumptions and the working capital needed to run at that capacity.

Risk

Cash flow, debt service & downside scenarios

What happens if volumes come in below plan — and whether the business can still service its debt.
