Investor Readiness Workshops

Help food founders prepare for their first investor conversation

Written for accelerators and program managers, not individual founders directly — workshop programming that gives early-stage food founders the investor lens and capital literacy most of them are missing.

What's covered

A deliberate path to a first investor cheque.

  • How investors read financial information
  • Capital instruments and investor types
  • Valuation and dilution
  • Simple cap tables
  • Angel-to-seed roadmaps
  • Non-dilutive versus dilutive funding, and building toward acquisition

We keep this programming separate from our work with established food SMEs — if you're an owner already running a scaling business, growth capital advisory is the right starting point instead.
